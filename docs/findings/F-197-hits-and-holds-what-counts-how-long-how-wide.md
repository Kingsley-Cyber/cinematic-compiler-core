## Description
1. Beyond a thrown or held blade landing in a body, do a blunt weapon, a grapple, a pin, a spell landing, a body thrown into a surface, or a touch outside combat owe a hold? 2. How long must a hit's hold last after the impact? 3. May a hit be held wide or extreme wide? 4. Is a smash cut, a whip onto the impact frame, a dissolve, or a shaky handheld shot a hold? 5. Does every printed impact frame get a hold (B's 5.50 throw, the 9.00 crash), or only the hits? 6. With three or more performers, does a hold frame every body present, and which pair sets the 180-degree axis? 7. Is a camera card's rule its printed words or its fields (F-187)?
