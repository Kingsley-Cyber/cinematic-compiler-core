ADR-O115. Every layer re-run on the envelope as it stood before it, every key it touches tracked
(`python -m validators.data_flow [envelope.json] [--dormant]`). R20's authored documents were not kept, so its authored
layers (except S17, whose document is rebuilt from its render node) are traced as far as the packet each sends its
author ("packet only"); the four examples, whose documents are committed, are traced whole.

Phantom: read, written by no layer in any envelope traced and declared by no schema (the layer guesses). Optional:
written in another envelope, absent in this one. Dead: written and read by no later layer (ids excluded). Dormant: read,
but changing it (on every item, and on the first alone) leaves what the layer writes the same.
