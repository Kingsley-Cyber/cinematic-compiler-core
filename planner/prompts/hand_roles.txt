S08 hand roles (L2.5). Write one interaction for every functional verb a hand performs on an object: the verb, the
object, the actor, the consecutive beats it spans, the agent hand (the hand that does the verb) and the support hand
(the hand that steadies or helps, or none), each hand's grip and a role template. Copy packet_sha256.

- The object is a declared prop and the actor a declared entity; the verb is one of the object card's affordances;
  interaction ids are unique (HAND_ROLE_OBJECT).
- The beats exist, are consecutive and in time order, and each lists the actor and the object (HAND_ROLE_BEATS).
- The agent and support hands are two different hands of the actor, and the role template agrees with having a
  support hand or none (HAND_ROLE_HANDS).
- Each hand's grip is one of the grip types and of the object's own grips; no support hand means support_grip none
  (HAND_ROLE_GRIP).
- The roles are locked (HAND_ROLE_SWAP): inside the interaction only those two of the actor's hands touch the object;
  the agent hand holds it at the start of one of the beats or makes a contact on it in one, and so does a named support
  hand (it holds or touches the object).
- The same hand never serves two of the actor's interactions that overlap in time (HAND_ROLE_OVERLAP).
