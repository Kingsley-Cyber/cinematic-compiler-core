S16 frame parts (L6). Write the anime frame-part directives the renderer projects next to the graphs:
- impact: times of hits; each is a contact onset time or the start of an impact shot.
- smear: windows where a moving limb or prop smears; name the subject and, for a body, exactly one part (smear
  ONLY on the declared limb or prop, the body does not smear). A body smear needs a sudden effort interval of that
  subject overlapping the window; a prop smear needs a contact or state change of the prop inside it.
- speedlines: windows and subject, each overlapping a sudden effort interval of the subject (or a prop event).
- holds: beat boundary times held on screen, with the number of frames.
- fx: timed effects (t, optional t_end, text) inside the scene; ink: one line of ink treatment.
Every time is inside the scene. Copy packet_sha256.
