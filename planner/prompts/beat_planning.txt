S04 beat planning (LLM call #1). Write the beat plan for this packet as JSON matching the beat_plan schema.

- Use only the entities, props and surfaces in the packet, by id; parts only from each item's parts.
- One actor per beat (an entity), plus participants it acts on. Times in seconds: t is the start, dur the length.
- Fit every beat inside the given duration, or inside the duration budget if none is given.
- For every entity in a beat (actor and entity participants) give root pos [x, y, z] and facing [x, y, z] in the
  scene axes at the beat start t (the key-pose time); these are authoritative for later steps.
- requires are read at t; effects and contacts commit at t + dur. Use only vocabulary properties and values.
- Contact types: touch (instant), possess (held or carried), support (bears weight), release (ends an earlier
  possess or support of the same pair).
- causes link this beat to a later beat it makes happen: hard (the later beat needs this beat's effect) or soft.
- No filler: a beat that is not a hold must change state, make or end a contact, or cause or be caused by another beat.
- modes: an entity's mode must be one of its card modes; a mode change needs a beat that causes it.
- Respect every constraint listed. Copy packet_sha256 into the plan. Do not invent ids, values or parts.
