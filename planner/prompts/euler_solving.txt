S13 motion (L4c). For every slot write one key pose: the body at the slot's beat start t.
- pos: copy the slot's S04 pos exactly (Q2). Do not move the root; if the anchor is wrong, the beat plan is re-authored.
- rot: root Euler degrees [X, Y, Z], intrinsic XYZ, right-handed (convention in the packet). Rest = upright, facing +Z,
  up +Y, +X = performer's left. +X pitches forward, +Y yaws left, +Z rolls clockwise (right shoulder down).
  Intrinsic XYZ applies X first, then Y about the new up axis, then Z: keep root X and Z at 0 outside flips and
  cartwheels and put leans in Sp, so Y alone sets the heading (a pitched root turns the heading; the check uses it).
- Heading: an upright root's forward axis must point along the slot's facing (within 1 degree). Mid-flip the lateral
  axis is read instead; mid-cartwheel the forward axis; a tipped root with no turn (slide, fall) uses the more
  horizontal of the two.
- Posture: with one allowed posture the root must show it (standing/crouching/kneeling upright, inverted upside
  down, prone/supine level, chest down/up); with several a change is under way and any in-between root is allowed.
- Angles are continuous per entity, never wrapped: the change from one key to the entity's next key is the rotation
  performed, sign = direction. A change of 90 degrees or more must be declared on the key where it starts:
  turn flip/axis X, spin/Y, cartwheel/Z; otherwise turn none, axis none.
- bones: every kernel joint, local Euler degrees from the rest pose, same convention. Segments that point down
  (LUL/RUL, LL/RL, LA/RA, LFA/RFA): +X swings them backward, -X forward (hip and shoulder flexion are -X, knee
  flexion is +X, elbow flexion is -X); +Z raises a left limb out to the left, -Z raises a right limb to the right.
  Segments that point up (Sp, NK, HD): +X tips forward, +Y turns left, +Z tilts to the right.
- Hip angles are read from the trunk (owner O84, rule R-35): LUL and RUL turn with the root and Sp, as the arms do,
  so a lean in Sp carries the legs with it. Feet that stay under a body leaning +X degrees in Sp need hips flexed
  by the same X (Sp [30,0,0] standing straight: LUL and RUL [-30,0,0]).
- Every joint's local Euler components stay inside packet.joint_limits (degrees per axis; "NK+HD" bounds the
  sum of the neck and head). Outside them the key is refused (JOINT_LIMIT).
- Read each slot's kinematic and Bartenieff nodes: the pose shows that support, loading and initiation.
- The world is measured by forward kinematics (packet segments, the body card's radii; owner O72's 0.15 m): a touch,
  hold or support meets its target at its instant (CONTACT_WORLD; the owner's exceptions come from the kernel list,
  never from this plan), and a contact between two of the actor's keys is measured on the pose the declared
  interpolation gives there (joints slerped, the root straight), its miss excused only while the span starts in the
  air (the key before claims no support) or its prop moves (ADR-O104); every support a slot's kinematic node claims meets its surface at that key, the part on the
  top its open support contact names or on the floor (CONTACT_WORLD, RV-14a); no two bodies overlap at a key they
  share (BODY_OVERLAP); no body point lies more than 0.15 m under the floor (packet floor_y) at any key
  (FLOOR_PENETRATION). Meet them with the bones; the root stays the slot's pos.
- name: a short snake_case pose name; description: one sentence. Copy packet_sha256.
