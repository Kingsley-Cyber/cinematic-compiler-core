S13 motion (L4c). For every slot write one key pose: the body at the slot's beat start t.
- pos: copy the slot's S04 pos exactly (Q2). Do not move the root; if the anchor is wrong, the beat plan is re-authored.
- rot: root Euler degrees [X, Y, Z], intrinsic XYZ, right-handed (convention in the packet). Rest = upright, facing +Z,
  up +Y, +X = performer's left. +X pitches forward, +Y yaws left, +Z rolls clockwise (right shoulder down).
  Intrinsic XYZ applies X first, then Y about the new up axis, then Z: keep root X and Z at 0 outside flips and
  cartwheels and put leans in Sp, so Y alone sets the heading (a pitched root turns the heading; the check uses it).
- Heading: an upright root's forward axis must point along the slot's facing (within 1 degree). Mid-flip the lateral
  axis is read instead; mid-cartwheel the forward axis; a tipped root with no turn (slide, fall) uses the more
  horizontal of the two.
- Posture: with one allowed posture the root must show it (standing/crouching/kneeling upright, inverted upside
  down, prone/supine level, chest down/up); with several a change is under way and any in-between root is allowed.
- Angles are continuous per entity, never wrapped: the change from one key to the entity's next key is the rotation
  performed, sign = direction. A change of 90 degrees or more must be declared on the key where it starts:
  turn flip/axis X, spin/Y, cartwheel/Z; otherwise turn none, axis none.
- words (PLAN-010): write each pose in the staff words the prompt prints; never joint angles. The compiler turns the
  words into the joints' angles (inside packet.joint_limits) and its solver then puts every touch, hold and support on
  its target. body: null when the trunk is upright, "inverted", or "<direction> (<n> lean)", e.g. "forward-high (30
  lean)"; head: null upright, or a direction; for each side, arm and leg: a direction, the limb's way from its joint
  in the body's frame (forward, left-forward, left, left-back, back, right-back, right, right-forward, each -high,
  -mid or -low, or place-high / place-low); lower_arm and lower_leg: "straight" or "folded <n>" (n in 15-degree steps).
  A limb that touches or stands on something needs only a near direction: the solver places its end.
- Read each slot's kinematic and Bartenieff nodes: the pose shows that support, loading and initiation.
- The world is measured by forward kinematics (packet segments, the body card's radii; owner O72's 0.15 m): a touch,
  hold or support meets its target at its instant (CONTACT_WORLD; the owner's exceptions come from the kernel list,
  never from this plan), and a contact between two of the actor's keys is measured on the pose the declared
  interpolation gives there (joints slerped, the root straight), its miss excused only while the actor has no support
  of its own open just before it, never at a landing (a support's onset), or while its prop moves (ADR-O104); every
  support a slot's kinematic node claims meets its surface at that key, the part on the
  top its open support contact names or on the floor (CONTACT_WORLD, RV-14a); no two bodies overlap at a key they
  share or at any frame between keys, each posed by the declared interpolation through its keys and in-betweens
  (BODY_OVERLAP, RV-59e); no body point lies more than 0.15 m under the floor (packet floor_y) at any key or any
  frame between keys
  (FLOOR_PENETRATION). The words set the pose, the solver meets them; the root stays the slot's pos.
- In-between keys (ADR-O109): where the declared interpolation would draw this body through another or a point more
  than 0.15 m under the floor, and no re-pose of the keys inside their limits clears it, add to `inbetweens` a pose of
  one entity at an instant strictly between two of its consecutive keys, on a frame no key or other in-between holds:
  entity, t, name, pos, rot, words (as a key's), description. It has no beat, contact or anchor; its root moves
  no faster than 25 m/s to its neighbours (TELEPORT); it is checked like a key (INBETWEEN_SPAN, JOINT_LIMIT, the floor,
  the bodies at its instant) and keeps one of the supports its keys on both sides claim within 0.15 m of its surface
  (INBETWEEN_SUPPORT). Densest around impacts and support changes, never every frame; a performer whose keys the
  source gives row by row takes none. Name and describe it from the pose it has, not the one meant.
- name: a short snake_case pose name; description: one sentence. Copy packet_sha256.
