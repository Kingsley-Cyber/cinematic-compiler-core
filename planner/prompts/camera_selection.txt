S16 camera (L5.5). Write the shot list: shots in time order from 0 to the scene end, no gaps, each listing the
beats it shows. Every shot fills all 13 subdivisions plus motivation; nothing is implicit.
- Motivation: one of beat_type (a shown beat id), entity_focus (a framed entity), contact_event (a contact id in
  the shot), effort_quality (an effort interval overlapping the shot), spatial_geometry (a surface or prop),
  narrative_intent (a causal edge touching a shown beat), genre_convention (a convention id of the genre card).
- Geometry is checked: the camera sits distance_m from the aim point at azimuth (heading from aim to camera, from +Z
  toward +X) and elevation. The aim point is the middle of the extent of the parts the shot's scale shows (the
  grammar's visible_parts) of its target at the shot's first beat, both axis performers for midpoint, read from the
  packet's `points` (the S13 keys); an insert aims aim_height_m above its target's pelvis. position.side must equal
  the derived side of the action axis; a cut that switches sides declares editing.axis cross.
- Show what matters: every shown beat's actor is framed; every contact onset in the shot is visible at the scale
  (the grammar's visible_parts, or the insert detail); every part the scale shows of each subject projects inside
  the frame, up and down as well as across, at every beat a static shot lists and at a moving shot's first beat
  (VERTICAL_FRAMING); composition placement agrees with the screen side of each subject's pelvis; frame height
  (24 mm x distance / focal) fits the scale. A body nearer the camera than the aim looks larger: fit tall or
  spread action with the distance (within the scale's frame height), a longer lens or the angle.
- Hits (owner O92, R-21, HIT_HOLD): the packet's `hits` are the contacts that hit (a touch on another performer's
  body; a strike, deflect or parry on a prop another performer holds or threw; a catch of a body; a body touched by a
  blade another performer holds). The shot covering each is static, with every body the hit lists among its subjects,
  never a close-up or an insert. Every shot plays at normal speed x1 (PLAYBACK_RAMP: no slow motion, ramp or freeze;
  the clip has a fixed length).
- Respect the grammar (rig movements and stability, lens perspective bands, 30-degree cut rule), the genre card
  (transitions, playback, rigs, roll, shot length) and the intent rules and tempo. Read the motion keys, efforts
  and idioms for what each moment needs. Copy packet_sha256.
- Faces (live action and UGC): a face whose salience is carries_beat must be shown in a shot listing its beat, at a
  scale that shows the head, turned toward the camera; if one side of it is stronger, that side faces the camera
  unless the shot is frontal.
