S16 camera (L5.5). Write the shot list: shots in time order from 0 to the scene end, no gaps, each listing the
beats it shows. Every shot fills all 13 subdivisions plus motivation; nothing is implicit.
- Motivation: one of beat_type (a shown beat id), entity_focus (a framed entity), contact_event (a contact id in
  the shot), effort_quality (an effort interval overlapping the shot), spatial_geometry (a surface or prop),
  narrative_intent (a causal edge touching a shown beat), genre_convention (a convention id of the genre card).
- Geometry is checked: the camera sits distance_m from the aim point (target position at the shot's first beat
  + aim height) at azimuth (heading from aim to camera, from +Z toward +X) and elevation. position.side must equal
  the derived side of the action axis; a cut that switches sides declares editing.axis cross.
- Show what matters: every shown beat's actor is framed; every contact onset in the shot is visible at the scale
  (the grammar's visible_parts, or the insert detail); every subject projects inside the frame; composition
  placement agrees with the projected screen side; frame height (24 mm x distance / focal) fits the scale.
- Hits (owner O92, R-21): a strike, deflect or blade that lands is held by a static camera with every body present
  in frame, never a close-up or an insert; every shot plays at normal speed x1 (no slow motion, ramp or freeze: the
  clip has a fixed length).
- Respect the grammar (rig movements and stability, lens perspective bands, 30-degree cut rule), the genre card
  (transitions, playback, rigs, roll, shot length) and the intent rules and tempo. Read the motion keys, efforts
  and idioms for what each moment needs. Copy packet_sha256.
- Faces (live action and UGC): a face whose salience is carries_beat must be shown in a shot listing its beat, at a
  scale that shows the head, turned toward the camera; if one side of it is stronger, that side faces the camera
  unless the shot is frontal.
